#### Here I will take notes of Linear Algebra concepts I find are important for QML. 

#### Bra and Kets
- Bra ```<x|``` - row vectors, written horizontally 
- Kets ```|x>``` - column vectors, written vertically

- Orthogonal - Perpendicular vectors
2 vectors are orthogonal iff - ```||a>|^2 + ||b>|^2 = ||a+b>|^2```

- Orthonormal - Perpendicular and normalized.

#### Transpose 
M^T - Interchanging the rows and columns
